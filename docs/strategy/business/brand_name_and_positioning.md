## The Positioning Statement

> **"Tension is an AI-powered relationship coaching app for couples who want to explore power dynamics, deepen intimacy, and build intentional connections."**

Notice what's NOT in there: "sex," "BDSM," "kink," "fetish."
Notice what IS: "relationship," "couples," "intimacy," "power dynamics," "intentional."

This positioning gets you into app stores, past payment processors, AND attracts exactly the right audience. The people who need this will understand "power dynamics" and "intentional intimacy." The people who don't will just see a relationship coaching app.

---

## Name Evaluation Framework

| Dimension | What You Want | What to Avoid |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| **App stores** | Sounds like a relationship/wellness app | Sounds sexual or adult |
| **Payment processors** | Legitimate coaching/education service | Adult entertainment |
| **Users** | Signals "this is for people like me" | Too clinical or too vanilla |
| **SEO** | Ranks for relationship + intimacy terms | Gets buried under porn |
| **Word of mouth** | "You should try this app" is easy to say | Awkward to recommend |

---

## Tier 1: Best Overall (Relationship + Subtle Kink Signal)

### 🏆 TENSION
- **Why it works:** Sexual tension, power tension, emotional tension — it's THE word for what you're building
- Feels like a premium wellness/lifestyle app
- Short, memorable, one word
- "We're working on our Tension" — easy to say
- Signals something deeper than "relationship app"
- App store: passes easily. Sounds like a meditation/mindfulness app.
- **THE WINNER**

### BOUND
- Double meaning — boundaries AND bondage
- People who know, know. People who don't, think "bound together"
- Short, powerful, one syllable
- "Bound helps couples explore deeper connection"
- App store: clean. It's about connection and boundaries.
- **RUNNER-UP**

### VELVET
- Soft, sensual, premium. Velvet handcuffs. Velvet rope.
- Feels luxury/lifestyle, not sex
- "Velvet — intimacy coaching for adventurous couples"
- Works as a feminine-leaning brand (both partners need to feel safe)
- App store: totally fine. Sounds like a beauty/lifestyle brand.

## Tier 2: Strong Contenders

### RITUAL
- D/s is built on rituals. Scenes are rituals. Protocols are rituals.
- "Ritual — intentional intimacy for couples"
- Signals intentionality and structure
- Clean, premium feel

### DYNAMIC
- Literally the word — "relationship dynamics," "power dynamics"
- "Dynamic — coaching for couples who want more"
- Professional, could pass as a general relationship app
- Downside: a bit generic

### ARC
- The arc of a scene. The arc of a relationship. The arc of growth.
- Short, modern, tech-startup feel
- "Arc — your intimacy journey, designed"
- Clean but maybe too abstract

## Tier 3: Bolder Positioning

### EMBER
- The spark that builds into fire
- "Ember — keep the spark alive, differently"
- Warm, not threatening

### PULSE
- Heartbeat, rhythm, energy
- "Pulse — rhythm of your relationship"
- Modern, health/wellness adjacent

### PACT
- Agreements, contracts, negotiations — core to kink
- "Pact — agreements that bring you closer"
- Clever but maybe too legal-sounding

## Names to NEVER Use

- ❌ Anything with "kink," "BDSM," "fetish," "dom," "sub" — instant app store rejection
- ❌ Anything with "sex" — payment processor nightmare
- ❌ Anything too abstract (won't rank in SEO)
- ❌ Anything too clinical (kills the vibe)

---

## Why Tension Wins

1. **App store?** Sounds like a wellness/mindfulness app. Easy approval.
2. **Payment processor?** "Tension helps couples build stronger relationships." Clean.
3. **SEO?** "Tension app couples" — low competition, easy to rank.
4. **Users?** People exploring kink FEEL tension. The name validates that feeling.
5. **Subtle signal?** Anyone in the lifestyle reads "Tension" and thinks "oh, I know what this is."
6. **Vanilla partner?** "I found this relationship app called Tension" — not threatening.
7. **Brandable?** Tension.build, GetTension.com — all work.
