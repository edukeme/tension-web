This post explains how the Tension blog layout works and what gets auto-generated for you.

## Main Section

This is a main section (h2). It will automatically appear in the Quick Navigation box at the top with a clickable link.

Write your content normally. Use paragraphs, bold, italic, lists — normal markdown.

- Bullet point 1
- Bullet point 2
- Bullet point 3

### Subsection

This is a subsection (h3). It also appears in Quick Navigation, indented under its parent h2.

You can use as many h2 and h3 headings as you need. They all become Quick Navigation links.

## Another Main Section

Start a new h2 for a new major topic.

The Quick Navigation automatically scans for these headings and creates a linked table of contents.

### Subsection of This Section

h3 headings indent under their parent h2.

## About the FAQ

The FAQ you defined in frontmatter renders at the bottom of the post. Readers see your questions and answers in a styled box.

## What Gets Auto-Generated

1. **Quick Navigation** — Links to all h2 and h3 headings
2. **FAQ** — Questions and answers from frontmatter (optional)
3. **Heading IDs** — Auto-generated for linking
4. **JSON-LD** — Article schema for SEO

You don't need to do anything for these. Just write, and the layout handles it.
