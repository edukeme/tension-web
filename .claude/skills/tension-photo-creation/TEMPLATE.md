# Photo Creation Template - Quick Reference

Copy and fill in for each new blog post image.

## Step 1: Choose Pose (Unique to this post)

Pick ONE couple interaction. Be specific:
- Hands intertwined
- One kneeling before the other  
- Standing embrace, faces almost touching
- One behind with hands on shoulders
- Leaning against wall with partner close
- Back-to-back, heads toward each other
- One hand on the other's face
- Figures bound loosely
- One guiding the other's chin up

**My choice:** ___________________________________

## Step 2: Choose Environment (Unique to this post)

Pick ONE ambient setting. Be specific about details:
- Luxury bedroom (hotel, silk sheets, soft lamps)
- Urban nightlife (rainy streets, streetlights, bokeh)
- High-end lounge (cocktail bar, amber shelves)
- Minimalist bedroom (clean lines, window light)
- Private villa (outdoor/indoor, luxury furniture)
- Penthouse (floor-to-floor windows, city lights)
- Sparse apartment (candlelight, few objects)
- Garden/outdoor (trees, moonlight)
- Luxury car (leather, interior lighting)

**My choice:** ___________________________________

## Step 3: Fill in the Prompt

```
A completely dark, high-contrast silhouette of [PASTE YOUR POSE HERE]. 
The background is heavily blurred and diffuse, set in [PASTE YOUR ENVIRONMENT HERE]. 
The entire image is bathed in deep, moody, burnt orange and dark crimson red lighting. 
Extreme shadows, cinematic low-light photography, premium erotic aesthetic, 
exploring intimacy and dominance fantasies tastefully without being explicit. 
Monochromatic warm amber-red color grading.
```

## Step 4: Generate Image

Use DALL-E, Midjourney, Ideogram, or your preferred AI image tool.

Paste the prompt above and generate.

## Step 5: Save Image

- **Filename:** `blog_[post-slug].png`
- **Location:** `public/images/`
- **Dimensions:** 1200x800px
- **Format:** PNG
- **Size:** <500KB

Example: `public/images/blog_bdsm-couples-test.png`

## Step 6: Verify

- [ ] Silhouettes are clear
- [ ] Lighting is burnt orange/crimson
- [ ] No explicit content
- [ ] Different from other blog post images
- [ ] File saved with correct name
